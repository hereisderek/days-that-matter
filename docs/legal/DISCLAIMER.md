## 4. What happens after a report

- We review each report. If it is valid, we act:
  - for a public link, we revoke the link, and its page shows "no longer available";
  - for a photo, we remove it from the day and delete the file;
  - for a custom audio file, we remove it from the day and delete the file. It stops playing in every app within 24 hours, and the day then shows no audio.
- A removed photo can stay reachable through caches for up to 1 day.
- We tell the user who added the content what we removed and why, unless the law forbids it.
- **Repeat infringers:** if we receive repeated valid copyright notices about files a user uploaded, we first block audio uploads for that account. If valid notices continue, about audio or photos, we close the account.

## 5. Counter-notices

If your content was removed after a copyright notice and you believe it was a mistake or a misidentification, you can send a counter-notice to our designated copyright agent (section 3). It must include:
1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the content that was removed and where it appeared before removal;
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith that the content was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address and phone number;
5. a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which we may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the copyright notice or their agent.

We send the counter-notice to the person who sent the notice. If they do not tell us within 10 to 14 business days that they have filed a court action, we let you add the content again, and the notice no longer counts toward the repeat-infringer policy.
