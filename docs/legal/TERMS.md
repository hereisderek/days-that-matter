## 6. Sharing

You decide what you share and with whom. Only the owner of a day can create and revoke its links and invites and manage its members.

**What is shared.** A shared day shows its shared fields: title, emoji, date, time, time zone, unit display, repeat setting, theme, backdrop photo and audio choice. Notes, pin state, category, archive state, reminders and memos are never shared.

**Public links.**
- Anyone who has a public link can see the day, in a browser or in the app, without an account. People can forward the link.
- Share pages are not listed in search engines, but a link is no longer private once you send it.
- A public link shows no names and no account details. Custom audio files never play on public links.
- Messaging apps may show a preview of a public link with the day's title, countdown and backdrop photo.
- A signed-in person who opens a public link in the app can save a copy of the day to their own account. Revoking the link does not remove copies already saved.
- You choose when a link expires (5 days, 30 days, never, or a date up to 5 years ahead) and can revoke it at any time.

**Invite links.**
- An invite adds people to the day as members, with the role you choose:
  - **viewer**: sees the day and its changes live;
  - **editor**: also changes the shared fields, backdrop and audio, and sees the edit history.
- You choose how many people can join with an invite (1 to 100, or unlimited) and when it expires. Anyone who has the invite link can join until it is used up, expires or is revoked.
- Joining happens in the app and needs an account. Before joining, a person who opens the invite in the app sees the day's title, your display name and the role.
- Members see the display names of the owner and the other members.

**Your controls as owner.** You can change a link's or invite's expiry, revoke links and invites, change a member's role, remove a member, and delete the day. A removed member cannot join again until you allow it. Members can leave a day at any time.

**When access ends.** When you revoke a link, remove a member or delete a day, open pages switch to "no longer available" and the day leaves the other person's app on its next sync. We cannot remove copies saved from a public link, screenshots, or previews that other apps already made. A removed photo can stay reachable through caches for up to 1 day.

**Editing someone else's day.** If you are an editor, your changes become part of the owner's day. If you leave the day or delete your account, your text changes stay in the day and its history (history then shows "Deleted user"). Photos and audio files you uploaded are removed from those days when you delete your account.
